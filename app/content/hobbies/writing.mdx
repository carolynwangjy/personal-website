i'm an alumna of the kenyon review's [young writers workshop](https://kenyonreview.org/high-school-workshops/) and umass amherst's [juniper institute for young writers](https://www.umass.edu/juniper-young-writers/) on a full-merit scholarship, which i attribute much of my creative work to. i am also quite proud of the saratoga falcon newspaper, which won a [silver crown](https://precollege.sps.columbia.edu/news/cspa-2024-crown-awards-finalists-news-publications) from the columbia scholastic press association during the year i led as editor-in-chief.

at uc berkeley, i am lucky enough to be a part of the university's advanced fiction workshop, studying under the tutelage of author [amy rowland](https://www.amyrowlandwriter.com/). 

my personal writing has been recognized by the scholastic art and writing awards (gold key), the new york times, the journalism education association of norcal, and national history day.
