## endurance sports

i've been involved with distance running for seven years now, starting in high school cross country and track (where i was varsity captain). i also raced on [cal triathlon](https://www.caltriathlon.com/), uc berkeley's club triathlon team (swim / bike / run) for two years!

nowadays, i primarily run for fun (although i'm on a hiatus due to injury), and am currently dabbling a little in strength training. i've written about some of my experiences [here](/writing/running-down-a-double-edged-sword) and [here](/writing/an-unexpected-close-captains-reflection-on-four-years-cross-country), and have been featured on the saratoga falcon [here](https://saratogafalcon.org/29022/sports/finding-fitness-at-uc-berkeley/) and [here](https://saratogafalcon.org/12047/sports/sophomore-dedicates-herself-pre-season-running/). below are some of the races i've done over the years and my favorite routes:

## music + performance

i'm currently taking vocal lessons with the wonderful [thomas truhitte](http://makeitatruehit.com/about.html)! my favorite pieces are musical theatre classics (les mis, chicago, phantom etc.) and broadway pieces for soprano ingénues. i also help organize [heartstrings](https://www.ocf.berkeley.edu/~heartstrings/), uc berkeley's music wellness club. if you like casual jam sessions, music performance, or community service, check us out :)

in the past, i did [flute](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLvUe5H_qmeidvCzgC6qQvUjjIj69AB4CQ&si=x2FPQU0wqvPphe6a) for six years (four years in symphony orchestra, two in band), piano for eight years, and classical voice for three years. i still put those hats on every now and then.

i am forever grateful to larisa meller and dr. ming li for nurturing my love for music. thank you both, for everything :)

## writing

i've always loved writing and always will! i most often [blog](/blog) nowadays, but i am getting back into [creative writing](/) (crossing my fingers on some new work coming out soon)!

i'm an alumna of the kenyon review's [young writers workshop](https://kenyonreview.org/high-school-workshops/) and umass amherst's [juniper institute for young writers](https://www.umass.edu/juniper-young-writers/) on a full-merit scholarship, which i attribute much of my creative work too.

i am also quite proud of the saratoga falcon newspaper, which won a [silver crown](https://precollege.sps.columbia.edu/news/cspa-2024-crown-awards-finalists-news-publications) from the columbia scholastic press association during the year i led as editor-in-chief.

my personal writing has been lucky enough to be recognized by the scholastic art and writing awards, the new york times, the journalism education association of norcal, and national history day.
